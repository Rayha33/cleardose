ClearDose privacy policy

Effective 5 September 2026

ClearDose is a medication reminder app. It is built so that your information never leaves your phone.

What ClearDose stores

Medicine names, strengths, instructions, dose times, the record of doses you mark as taken or skipped, optional photos you attach, and an optional family contact name and phone number. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, and in your iCloud device backup if you have backups turned on.

What ClearDose does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No servers. The app makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is Apple's App Store handling the optional one-time purchase.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No access to Apple Health.

Sharing you control

When you tap "Send report to family" or "Text family", ClearDose opens the iOS share sheet or Messages with the text pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you send it, and the app never sends anything on its own.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose photo confirmation, and the photo library only when you add a picture of a pill. Photos are stored on the device and can be deleted by deleting the dose or the medicine.

Deleting your data

Delete a medicine to remove it and its history. Delete the app to remove everything.

Medical disclaimer

ClearDose is a reminder tool, not a medical device and not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor and pharmacist.
